I’m Jan Nicole Nieves, a Filipino illustrator and animator based in Toronto. I graduated from Ateneo de Naga University with a BS in Digital Illustration & Animation.

My practice moves between character art, frame-by-frame animation, and storyboarding. I’m drawn to expressive characters and stories that carry a little of the world they come from.
